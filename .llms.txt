# Afive Music Publishing

> Stockholm-based music publishing company representing songwriters and composers.

## About

Afive Music Publishing is a modern music publishing company dedicated to representing talented songwriters and composers. We manage music catalogs, handle royalty collection, and provide sync licensing opportunities for film, TV, advertising, and other media.

## Services

- **Music Publishing**: Comprehensive rights management and royalty collection
- **Sync Licensing**: Placement opportunities in film, TV, advertising, and games
- **Catalog Management**: Professional administration of musical works
- **Creative Support**: Resources and guidance for songwriters and composers

## Technology Stack

- **Framework**: Next.js 16.3.6 (React 19.2.4)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS 4
- **Animations**: GSAP, Motion, Lenis
- **Fonts**: Custom fonts including Chillax, Satoshi, General Sans, Bespoke Serif, New Title
